2. Around lunch — Unimate again

A second Unimate around lunch keeps the same routine going through the middle of the day.

3. Biggest meal — Balance

Take Balance with your biggest meal, most often dinner. It is a fiber-matrix drink that supports satiety and healthy blood sugar already in the normal range.

4. After dinner — the window closes

Stop eating after dinner, and keep the break until the next day. Most people using this routine use around a 16:8 eating window. The point is consistency, not perfection on day one.
