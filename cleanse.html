Cleanse, component by component

Paraway Plus

Herbal gastrointestinal support to rebalance the gut microbiome, as presented. Herbal blend including black walnut hull, pumpkin seed, clove flower bud, and garlic bulb. Two capsules each morning for the first 10 days, then five each morning for the next 20 days, with food, never more than five daily. Not for long-term use — only once or twice a year over a 30-day period, as presented.

LiFiber

Psyllium-husk fiber drink with a proprietary herbal blend, as presented, to nourish the digestive system and promote regularity. Banana flavour. One level scoop in 8–10 oz water, 1–2 times daily.

Aloe Vera

Gentle, short-term capsule to support digestive regularity, as presented. One capsule daily with a full glass of water. Explicitly not for long-term use and not more than one per day, as presented — and not with diarrhea, loose stools, or abdominal pain. Talk to a physician if pregnant, nursing, medicated, or with a medical condition.
