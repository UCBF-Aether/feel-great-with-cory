The way I roll · Not starving, timing

Fasting, plain and simple.

Fasting isn't punishment. It's a window. You stop eating after dinner, you give your body a break, you start again the next day — and you track what happens instead of guessing.

What fasting actually is here

16 hours not eating, 8 hours eating. For example: last meal at 8 p.m., first meal at noon the next day. That's 16 hours. Then you eat between noon and 8 p.m. That's your 8 hours.

Some Unicity materials are looser, at 14–16 hours. The point is the same: a break, every day, not a starvation week.

What breaks a fast, in plain words

As Unicity presents it:

✅ Does not break the fast: water, and Unimate — including Decaf — which Unicity presents as fasting-friendly. That's why Unimate goes in the morning and at lunch, while you're still in the fast.

🛑 Breaks the fast / belongs in the window: Balance, because it's taken with your biggest meal, and any food, snack, or sugary drink. If it has calories that tell your body "we're eating now," it's window food, not fast food.

For other products, follow the pack label and ask your provider if you're unsure — Unicity's own materials disagree on a couple of edge cases, so this site doesn't publish a single list as settled.

What to drink during the fast

Water first, always. Unimate morning and lunch, as above. Signal, Unicity's hydration drink, is also presented as fasting-friendly, sugar-free and caffeine-free. Keep it simple: water + Unimate, and don't overcomplicate the fast with a dozen other drinks.

The other things that go with it — how I roll

Track, don't guess. That's the whole map: your numbers, your baseline, your 90-day tracker. Get the tracker →

Walk. No gym speech, no 10,000-step rule here — just move every day, in a way you can actually keep.

Sleep. A broken window is hard to keep if you're up all night snacking. The window closes after dinner for a reason — sleep is part of the fast, not separate from it.

Water and salt sense. Drink water through the fast, and if you feel dizzy, lightheaded, or unwell, stop and talk to your provider. Fasting is not supposed to feel like punishment.

Don't do it alone if you're unsure. Talk to me in the Hub, or talk to your provider first — especially if you're pregnant, nursing, on medication, underweight, or have a medical condition. Unicity's clinician materials flag time-restricted eating as not suiting everyone.

Common mistakes

❌ Skipping Balance and calling it "extra fasting." Balance is part of the routine, with your biggest meal — don't drop it to chase a longer fast.

❌ One bad day = quit. Miss one day, keep going. Don't miss two in a row, as Unicity's materials put it.

❌ Guessing instead of tracking. If you don't write it down, you're back in the loop. That's why the tracker exists.
