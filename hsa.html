HSA / FSA

Can you pay with HSA or FSA? As presented in the files.

Yes — Feel Great is HSA / FSA eligible

You're right. Unicity's own Help Center says it plainly: all Feel Great packs are eligible for FSA and HSA payments (one-time purchases only), and add-on packs added after a Feel Great pack are also eligible.

How it works

  1. At checkout, select HSA/FSA as your payment method (one-time purchase orders only — this option is not for subscriptions).
  2. Complete the Truemed survey. A licensed provider reviews it and, if you qualify, issues a Letter of Medical Necessity.
  3. Pay with your HSA/FSA card, or pay with a regular card and submit for reimbursement using the letter Truemed sends you.

Good to know: a Letter of Medical Necessity is valid for 12 months, so you do not need to redo the survey every time while yours is still valid. Truemed's own timing in the library files is generally 20 minutes to 36 hours, with a 7% service charge, US only, not Puerto Rico.

Not a guarantee for you personally: eligibility depends on Truemed's provider review and your plan's rules — check with your HSA/FSA administrator if you're unsure. Sources: Unicity Help Center — HSA/FSA FAQ and Truemed — Feel Great.
